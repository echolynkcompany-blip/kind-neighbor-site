# The Kind Neighbor Co. website

A static, mobile-friendly landing page for a prelaunch Zephyrhills, Florida homemaker and companion service.

## Files
- `index.html`: all page content, links, and SEO metadata
- `styles.css`: responsive layout and brand colors
- `script.js`: footer year
- `assets/logo.png`: brand logo
- `.nojekyll`: tells GitHub Pages to serve files without Jekyll processing

## Before publishing
1. Replace `hello@example.com` in `index.html` with your actual business email. Do not publish the example email address.
2. Confirm final services and pricing. Pricing shown is a proposal, not a market-validated guarantee.
3. Confirm Florida business-name rights and registration status; do not imply the business is registered or accepting clients before appropriate authorization.
4. Add your AHCA registration number to website advertising once issued and before marketing services, as required.
5. Replace prelaunch language with accurate operating status when authorized.
6. Consider a custom domain after verifying business name and domain availability.

## Free deployment with GitHub Pages (no command line)
1. Sign in to github.com, click **New repository**.
2. Name it `kind-neighbor-site`, set visibility to **Public**, then create it. GitHub Pages on the free plan supports public repositories.
3. In the repository choose **Add file → Upload files**. Upload `index.html`, `styles.css`, `script.js`, `.nojekyll`, and the `assets` folder contents preserving the `assets/logo.png` path. A simpler alternative is to unzip the archive locally and drag all extracted contents into GitHub's upload interface; verify that the logo ends up at `assets/logo.png`.
4. Commit the files to the default `main` branch.
5. Go to **Settings → Pages**. Under **Build and deployment**, choose **Deploy from a branch**, select `main`, folder `/(root)`, and save.
6. Wait for the Pages deployment to complete. Your website will be at `https://YOUR-GITHUB-USERNAME.github.io/kind-neighbor-site/` (replace username with yours).
7. Test the page on a phone, including service links and the email button.

## Connect Google later
- In Google Business Profile, add the final website URL once the business is eligible and verified. For a home-based service-area business, hide the residential address from public display when you do not receive customers there.
- In Google Search Console, verify your site and request indexing.
- Keep name, service area, and contact information consistent between the website and Google Business Profile.
- GitHub Pages is public static hosting. Do not store client records, private inquiries, passwords, or secrets in the repository. Add a secure third-party contact/booking tool later if needed.
