The Kind Neighbor Co.
Zephyrhills, Florida.

The Kind Neighbor Co. was created around a simple idea: sometimes an extra pair of hands makes a meaningful difference.

We welcome individuals and families, including busy parents, stay-at-home parents, single moms and dads, and households that want help getting a project done. We welcome people of all genders, identities, and backgrounds.

Our approach is practical and respectful: listen to your priorities, agree on a realistic task list, and communicate clearly about the work.

Local and personal.

We serve Zephyrhills, Florida. Availability and travel limits are confirmed for each request. Ask about weekday and Saturday availability when requesting your quote.

Our current service menu covers household tasks. We do not offer home health care, hands-on personal care, medication assistance, childcare, supervision, respite care, or passenger transportation. Requests involving senior or disability-related homemaker or companion services require review of applicable registration requirements before acceptance.
