Companion services in Zephyrhills, Florida.

A friendly visit can bring conversation, shared interests and a familiar presence to the week. The Kind Neighbor Co. plans to offer nonmedical companionship for adults and older neighbors in Zephyrhills beginning in December 2026.

What can a companion visit include?

  • Conversation and social time: talk about the day, look through photos or enjoy a favorite program together.
  • Hobbies and games: share a card game, reading, puzzles or another agreed activity.
  • Everyday technology help: practice making a video call, finding a contact or using familiar phone features. You stay in control of your accounts and passwords.
  • Outing companionship: discuss company for an appointment, grocery trip or everyday errand. Outings and travel arrangements must be agreed in advance; joining the waitlist does not reserve transportation.
  • Company while family takes a break: arrange social time where the person’s needs fit a nonmedical companion visit.

What might a visit look like?

A visit could start with a conversation about the week, continue with a favorite game, and finish with help setting up a family video call. Another person might prefer a quiet visit and company while making a grocery list. We’ll discuss preferences rather than assume everyone wants the same routine.

Arranging support for a parent or family member

You can join the waitlist while exploring support for someone else. Before booking, we’ll discuss the person’s preferences, the requested activities and whether our planned services are a suitable fit. The visit plan and available schedule will be agreed before services begin.

Where companionship ends

Companionship is nonmedical support. Our planned services do not include bathing, dressing, toileting, transfers, medication administration or medical care. We do not promise continuous supervision or emergency monitoring. If hands-on personal care is needed, these companion visits are not a substitute for that care.

Companionship or help around the home?

If your main concern is laundry, light housekeeping or preparing meals, explore our planned homemaker services. We can discuss a visit plan that reflects the agreed priorities and time available.
