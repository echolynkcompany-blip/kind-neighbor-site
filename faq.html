A few helpful answers.

Can I contact you now?

Yes. We are accepting household-service inquiries now. We review each request before accepting work. A request is not a confirmed appointment.

Who can request help?

Individuals and families of all backgrounds are welcome, including stay-at-home parents, single parents, and busy households. Service eligibility depends on the actual tasks and any applicable requirements, not just how the service is described.

What does it cost?

We provide a quote after discussing the tasks, expected time, supplies, and location. The price and any expenses are agreed before you book. This website does not collect payments.

Do you provide senior care or home health services?

We are not offering home health, hands-on personal care, medication assistance, supervision, or respite care. Requests that fall within regulated homemaker or companion services are not accepted unless applicable requirements have been met.

Can you cook, shop, or drive me somewhere?

Meal preparation, shopping assistance, accompanied outings, and passenger transportation are not on our current menu. We are focusing on the household tasks listed on the Services page.

Can you help with a cluttered home?

We can discuss ordinary sorting, organization, and garage-sale preparation. We do not offer hoarding treatment, biohazard cleanup, mold remediation, pest cleanup, heavy hauling, or unsafe work.

Do you watch children while helping parents?

No. Household assistance does not include childcare or supervision. A responsible adult must remain responsible for children.

What if I joined the previous waitlist?

Your earlier submission is not a confirmed appointment. Contact us to discuss the current household-service menu and update your request; submitting this new form does not erase your earlier record.

How do I change or cancel a visit?

Call 813-904-2995 as soon as possible. Any applicable cancellation terms will be provided before you confirm a booking.

How is my information used?

We use inquiry information to review requests, contact you, and manage agreed services. Read our Privacy policy for details and privacy contact options.
