Your questions, answered.

Planning ahead for everyday support in Zephyrhills? Here is how our December 2026 launch and free priority waitlist work.

When will The Kind Neighbor Co. open?

We plan to open in December 2026. An exact opening day has not been announced. Waitlist members will receive opening and booking updates.

Which areas will you serve?

Our planned service area is Zephyrhills, Florida. We’ll confirm whether your location and requested schedule can be accommodated before booking. We are not promising service throughout Pasco County.

What services are planned?

We are preparing companionship and social visits, light housekeeping and laundry, meal preparation and grocery planning, everyday organization, technology assistance, and agreed errands or outing companionship. Every visit needs an agreed plan.

Do you provide personal care or medical care?

No. Our planned services are nonmedical. We do not provide bathing, dressing, toileting, transfers, medication administration or medical care. We do not offer emergency response or continuous monitoring.

How much will visits cost?

Final prices are not published on this prelaunch website. We’ll discuss the requested services, visit length and price before you agree to a booking. Joining the waitlist is free and does not require payment.

Can I choose days and times now?

The waitlist records your preferred visit frequency and length. Actual dates and times will be arranged when booking opens, subject to availability. A place on the list does not guarantee a particular schedule.

What happens after I join the waitlist?

Your priority booking request is saved. We’ll contact people in sign-up order when booking opens to discuss needs, service fit, pricing and scheduling. Joining is an expression of interest, not a confirmed visit or service agreement.

Can I join for a parent or relative?

Yes. Use your own name and email if you will be the contact. Please do not submit health information through the waitlist form. We’ll discuss the intended recipient’s preferences and requested support before booking.

Will you drive clients to appointments?

We are planning appointment and outing companionship, but transportation is not guaranteed. Travel arrangements and the requested outing must be discussed and agreed before booking.

Can I request occasional or recurring help?

The form lets you express interest in a one-time visit or recurring visits and select a preferred length from one to four hours. These are preferences for planning; the final visit length and frequency depend on the agreed service plan and availability.

What if I have already joined or want to leave?

Joining again keeps your original queue place. To update your details or leave the list, email echolynkcompany@gmail.com. No payment is collected to join.
