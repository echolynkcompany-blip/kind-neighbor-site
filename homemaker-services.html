Homemaker services in Zephyrhills, Florida.

Everyday household tasks can add up. The Kind Neighbor Co. is preparing nonmedical homemaker support in Zephyrhills, with a planned December 2026 opening. Our focus is practical help with ordinary routines and a task list agreed with you.

Light housekeeping and laundry

Planned support includes everyday tidying, wiping accessible surfaces, washing dishes and helping with laundry or folding. Before booking, we’ll agree on the tasks, priorities and time needed. This is light household assistance; deep cleaning, heavy lifting and hazardous cleanup are outside the planned service.

Meal preparation and grocery planning

A visit could include making a grocery list, helping organize everyday ingredients or preparing a simple meal according to your preferences and the agreed plan. Tell us about relevant food preferences and restrictions during the planning conversation. We do not provide clinical nutrition advice or medical dietary management.

Home organization and everyday routines

Help could include sorting a small everyday area, putting away laundry or organizing frequently used items where you prefer them. You decide what stays and where things belong. This is everyday organization rather than a whole-home clearance or specialist cleanup service.

Errands and grocery support

Discuss the grocery shopping or everyday errands you would like help with. The task, timing, purchases and any travel arrangements need to be confirmed before a visit. We do not promise transport availability through the waitlist.

A practical example

A household might prioritize a load of laundry, a kitchen tidy and preparation for lunch. We’ll discuss whether the requested work fits the visit length rather than promise that every task can be completed in one visit. Priorities can be agreed before booking.

Who might find this helpful?

Homemaker support may suit an older adult who wants help with household routines, an adult seeking practical assistance, or a family arranging everyday help for a relative. Suitability depends on the tasks and support needed; we’ll discuss that before confirming services.

Nonmedical support with clear boundaries

We do not provide hands-on personal care, medication administration, nursing or medical services. If you are looking primarily for conversation, shared activities or company for a family member, read about our planned companionship services.

Visit length and pricing

The waitlist lets you express a preferred visit length and frequency. Final pricing, tasks and available times will be discussed before booking. There is no charge to join the list and no payment is collected through the waitlist form. See our launch and booking FAQ for more details.
