Our approach

Here to help, happy to stay.

At The Kind Neighbor Co., we believe that practical help and a friendly conversation can make a meaningful difference. Our goal is to provide familiar, respectful nonmedical assistance to adults and families in the Zephyrhills community.

We're building a local, owner-operated service around clear communication, consistent visits, and each person's preferences and independence.

Service boundaries: We do not offer bathing, dressing, toileting, lifting or transfers, medication administration, nursing, or other medical and hands-on personal care through homemaker and companion services.

Questions families ask

Frequently asked questions

Where do you provide services?

Our initial service area is Zephyrhills, Florida. Availability depends on the location and requested schedule.

When are visits available?

Monday–Friday, 7 a.m.–3 p.m., and Saturday, 8 a.m.–1 p.m. We are closed Sundays. All times are Eastern Time. Visits must finish within these hours. Availability depends on existing visits, your location, and travel time; submitting an inquiry does not reserve a time.

Can I combine services during one visit?

Yes. Companionship, light housekeeping, meal preparation, and other eligible nonmedical tasks can be combined within the booked time.

Do you provide nursing or personal care?

No. Our homemaker and companion services do not include hands-on personal care or medical services.

Do you transport clients?

Errands and appointment accompaniment may be arranged when appropriate transportation and insurance arrangements are in place.
