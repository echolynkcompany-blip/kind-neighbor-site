Privacy policy

Effective October 30, 2026. This policy covers the public website at thekindneighbor.site and the household-service inquiries submitted through it.

Information we collect

When you send a request, we collect your name, phone number, optional email, ZIP code, selected tasks, requested frequency and start timing, relationship to the person requesting help, contact permission, and any preferences or message you provide. Our operations system records the request and submission time. Please do not include health records, identification numbers, payment card details, or passwords.

How we use it

We use this information to respond, review service scope and availability, prepare quotes, manage agreed work, keep business records, and protect our forms from spam or abuse. Contact permission on the inquiry form is for responding to that request; it is not a blanket marketing subscription.

Service providers and technical information

The public website is hosted through GitHub Pages. Requests are sent over HTTPS to our operations application hosted on Railway. These providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser information, and access logs under their own policies. Authorized people working on our behalf may access information needed to operate the business. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or to protect rights and security.

Browser storage and campaign links

The inquiry script uses session storage to remember when you arrive through a Google Business Profile campaign link, so that source can accompany your request. Session storage is normally cleared when the browser tab or session ends. You can clear it through browser settings. The current public pages do not load advertising trackers or an optional analytics script. Our anti-spam form uses a temporary server-issued challenge.

Retention and your choices

We retain records as needed to respond to requests, manage services, meet applicable recordkeeping duties, and resolve issues. You may ask to access, correct, or delete information by contacting us. Some records may need to be retained for legal or business obligations. We do not sell inquiry information.

Security and children

We use HTTPS for the website and inquiry connection. No electronic system can guarantee absolute security. The request form is intended for adults; please do not submit personal information about children.

Contact and changes

For privacy requests, email hello@thekindneighbor.site or call 813-904-2995. You may also contact echolynkcompany@gmail.com. We will update this page and its effective date when our website practices change.
