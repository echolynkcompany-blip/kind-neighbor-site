Our current service menu

Practical help for your home.

Choose a single project or ask about recurring household help. We tailor the task list and quote to the work you request.

Cleaning & tidying

A fresher home, without the full to-do list.

Dusting, vacuuming, mopping, kitchen and bathroom cleaning, dishes, and general tidying. Agree on rooms and priorities before we arrive.

Laundry & fresh linens

Make room for a lighter week.

Washing, drying, folding, putting away laundry, and changing unoccupied bed linens using agreed household supplies.

Home organization

Find a place for the everyday things.

Closets, pantries, cabinets, storage areas, and household paperwork sorting. You decide what to keep, donate, or discard.

Decluttering & garage-sale preparation

Turn an overwhelming project into smaller steps.

Help sorting belongings, labeling and arranging items, setting up a garage sale, and boxing donations. No hazardous cleanup, heavy hauling, or hoarding treatment.

Packing & household projects

An extra pair of hands for a practical task.

Packing and unpacking household belongings, seasonal organization, and setting up simple storage systems. No construction or licensed trade work.

Everyday technology help

Patient explanations, at your pace.

Help learning phone settings, video calls, email, and everyday apps. You stay in control of passwords, accounts, and financial decisions.

Our current service menu covers household tasks. We do not offer home health care, hands-on personal care, medication assistance, childcare, supervision, respite care, or passenger transportation. Requests involving senior or disability-related homemaker or companion services require review of applicable registration requirements before acceptance.

We confirm whether your address is within our service area before accepting a request. Tell us about pets, supplies, stairs, and access needs when we discuss your quote.
