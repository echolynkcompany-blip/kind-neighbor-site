Service terms

Effective October 30, 2026.

Requests and booking

Submitting a form asks us to review a request. It does not reserve a date, create a service agreement, guarantee acceptance, or authorize a payment. We confirm the scope, location, availability, quote, and payment and cancellation terms before booking.

Household-service scope

Only the tasks expressly agreed for your visit are included. Work outside the agreed scope requires approval. We may decline tasks outside our capabilities, applicable requirements, or safe working conditions.

Our current service menu covers household tasks. We do not offer home health care, hands-on personal care, medication assistance, childcare, supervision, respite care, or passenger transportation. Requests involving senior or disability-related homemaker or companion services require review of applicable registration requirements before acceptance.

Access, supplies, and belongings

Tell us about access restrictions, pets, stairs, fragile belongings, and known hazards before the visit. Supplies, equipment, disposal arrangements, and any approved expenses must be agreed in advance. You decide which belongings are kept, donated, sold, or discarded; we require your authorization before removing items.

Changes and cancellations

Contact us as soon as your plans change. Any applicable fees or notice requirements will be disclosed before booking. This website itself imposes no cancellation charge.
